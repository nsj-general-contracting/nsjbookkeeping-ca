Before we get started

Common questions.
Clear answers.

Do I need to be in Regina?

No. NSJ is based in Regina and works virtually with businesses in Saskatoon and throughout Saskatchewan, as well as elsewhere in Canada.

My books are months behind. Can I still contact you?

Yes. Tell us which periods are outstanding and what records you have. We’ll review the situation and discuss the catch-up work needed.

How much does bookkeeping cost?

Pricing depends on your transaction volume, the condition of your records and the support you need. We discuss the scope and pricing with you before work begins.

What should I have ready for our first conversation?

Your business type, the bookkeeping software you use (if any), the periods you need help with and a rough idea of your monthly transaction volume. Please don’t send bank statements, passwords or other sensitive documents through the inquiry form.

How do I request a consultation?

Complete the short form below and select Send My Inquiry. Your request goes directly to NSJ’s inbox, and a confirmation appears on this page. You can also email nsjfinancialservices@gmail.com directly. No account is needed.

Do I need to commit before speaking with you?

No. The initial inquiry helps us understand your needs and discuss whether the service is a good fit. You decide whether to proceed after discussing the scope and pricing.
